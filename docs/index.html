PDFGist

The AI PDF reader that brings your own model.

Most AI PDF apps lock you into their subscription. PDFGist is a thin, fast reader, and the intelligence is whatever model you point it at: any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — OpenAI, DeepSeek, Kimi, SiliconFlow, Ollama (local) — with your key stored on your machine and requests sent straight to your provider.

Screenshot placeholder — reader view with AI sidebar streaming a translation.

Highlights

  • Select any text, one click to translate — results stream in as they generate
  • One-click structured summaries of the current page, a selection, or the whole document
  • Chat with the document, grounded in the current page, your selection, or the first pages
  • Presets for DeepSeek, OpenAI, Moonshot Kimi, SiliconFlow and Ollama, or any custom base URL
  • Continuous-scroll reading with zoom, fit-width, document outline and keyboard navigation
  • Local-first: no account, no telemetry, keys in your user config directory
