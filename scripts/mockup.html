Attention Is All You Need: A Reproducibility Study

Abstract — We present the Transformer, a sequence model built entirely on attention, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely.

The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks that include an encoder and a decoder. The best performing models also connect the encoder and decoder through an attention mechanism.

We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms. Experiments on two machine translation tasks show these models to be superior in quality while being more parallelizable and requiring significantly less time to train.

An attention function can be described as mapping a query and a set of key-value pairs to an output, where the query, keys, values, and output are all vectors. The output is computed as a weighted sum of the values.
